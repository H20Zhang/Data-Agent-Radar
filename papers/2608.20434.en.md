# MAESTRO: Connect Materials Discovery into a Traceable Computational Loop

[中文](2608.20434.md) | **English** · [Home](../README.en.md) · [Research Library](../library/README.en.md)

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20434) · **Importance: 4/5**

> **Research delta.** MAESTRO connects literature retrieval, crystal-structure cleaning, property computation, multi-fidelity screening, and candidate delivery through typed messages and shared state, while persisting agent decisions for replay. Deterministic scientific tools still perform the numerical work.

## Problem

Computational materials discovery is commonly split into literature, structure, descriptors, screening, process simulation, and physical validation. Manual handoffs lose provenance, assumptions, and failures and are hard to rerun when new data arrive.

## Mechanism

One Claude Opus 4.6 orchestrator coordinates Literature, Structure, Property, and Screening specialists. Every stage uses `plan → execute → reflect → gate` with typed messages and shared state. Structure checkers, Widom insertion, GCMC, VSA optimization, and MLP-MD perform the core computation. Context, reasoning, and decisions are stored as JSON.

## Closest comparison

CALF-20 is the external commercial-material baseline for the scientific result. There is no matched single-agent, deterministic pipeline, human workflow, or reflection/gate ablation for the agent mechanism. The study shows that the MAESTRO package completed a campaign, not that multi-agent or LLM orchestration caused the outcome.

## Decisive evidence

- More than 400,000 papers and 190,000 structure variants enter the pipeline; strict intersection of five structure tools retains **64,097** computation-ready entries.
- The screening funnel is 64,071 → 19,207 → 1,035 → 280 → 205 → 11. Ten of 11 final candidates meet the three computational criteria for dry purity, wet retention, and hydrophobicity.
- At RH=1, the 11 candidates retain 93.7–98.8% of dry CO₂ capacity, versus 6.5% for CALF-20. These are simulations, not experimental adsorption or transport validation.
- Full process simulation changes proxy rankings: YIWBEN moves from 10th under equilibrium VSA to 2nd in the complete process, while BELCUT falls from 1st to 6th.
- A three-month incremental run processes roughly 300 new CIFs without manual intervention. The paper shows one selected self-correction, not a population recovery rate.

## Cost and failure boundary

The literature audit estimates only 77% precision from 100 accepted and 100 rejected samples. Five-tool intersection raises confidence while sacrificing recall. The campaign includes 43,200 process points and 12,046 MLP-MD frames, but omits total tokens, API cost, wall time, and hardware.

One model makes all LLM decisions; prompts, inference parameters, and complete tool versions are incomplete. Deterministic tools and traces support reproducible execution and artifact lineage, but rigid-framework assumptions, classical force fields, and screening thresholds still limit scientific truth. Candidates require synthesis, characterization, and transport experiments.

## Claim ceiling

A traced LLM-controlled package successfully links large-scale literature/structure curation with multi-fidelity simulation and yields convergent computational candidates. The evidence does not isolate multi-agent design, reflection/gates, or LLM planning, and does not demonstrate experimentally effective materials.

## Field-map consequence

This is an `early_signal` in **Execution & Transformation**, connected to Data Discovery & Grounding and Verification & Recovery. It shows that complex data campaigns can share traceable state; one package result does not change the stable map.
