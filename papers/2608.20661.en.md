# KDAF: Make Enterprise-Analytics Evidence Paths Replayable

[中文](2608.20661.md) | **English** · [Home](../README.en.md) · [Research Library](../library/README.en.md)

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20661) · **Importance: 3/5**

> **Research delta.** KDAF / CARP does not treat a plausible answer as the only objective. It enforces a company-entity boundary during retrieval and records relation types, confidence, selection traces, and complete provenance chains for selected evidence.

## Problem

Enterprise-finance QA may retrieve semantically similar passages from the wrong company or period. Even a fluent answer can be hard to audit: why was evidence selected, which filing and page supplied it, and can the decision be replayed later?

## Mechanism

KDAF proposes a six-stage ontology method; the empirical study exercises graph construction, CARP retrieval, and audit. Company, concept, and period seeds drive multiplicative propagation, with company as a hard eligibility boundary. Final ranking mixes lexical score, propagation, concept coverage, and period coverage. Every traversal proposal records an accept/reject reason, and selected evidence keeps a `company → filing → page → evidence` chain.

## Closest comparison

BM25 is the strongest simple retrieval control over the same candidate pool; `graph_no_ontology` is the closest ontology ablation. The decisive missing control is sparse retrieval with an explicit company metadata filter, which would test whether zero cross-company leakage requires an ontology or just a simple filter.

## Decisive evidence

- The frozen FinanceBench slice contains 145 non-calibration questions. KDAF correctness is **0.110**, compared with **0.117** for BM25; the difference is −0.007 with 95% CI [−0.021, 0.000]. Graph structure does not improve answer correctness.
- Citation traceability F1 is **0.515**, versus 0.488 for an ungrounded graph and 0.463 for BM25. The paired KDAF–BM25 difference is +0.052, 95% CI [+0.024, +0.083].
- Cross-company leakage is **0/426** KDAF evidence items, compared with 73/435 (16.8%) for BM25 and 88/435 (20.2%) for the lexical hybrid. KDAF still selects off-period evidence in 70/341 cases (20.5%).
- Replay matches in 145/145 questions, and selected evidence always resolves to a complete chain. The paper correctly limits this to serialization completeness, not independently reconstructed factual correctness.

## Cost and failure boundary

The evaluated graph contains 524 nodes and 1,852 edges at about 29 MB. KDAF retrieval p50 is 4.14 ms, but its mean context is 1,581 tokens—larger than the lexical hybrid—so there is no compactness gain. Ontology and expert-construction costs are unquantified.

The candidate pool is derived from gold evidence and contains only 189 evidence records for 145 questions, not open full-filing retrieval. One Qwen3-8B no-think generator is used, without dense or published GraphRAG controls. The competency-question, expert-graph, and human-validation stages are not evaluated.

Complete provenance is not business truth. Overall correctness is low, period evidence still drifts, the FP&A variance scenario is design-only, and no real business definitions or recovery loop are tested.

## Claim ceiling

In this controlled financial-QA candidate pool, ontology-grounded retrieval improves citation attribution and company-entity discipline without improving answer correctness. The evidence supports an auditability design point, not operational FP&A correctness or a causal advantage over a simple entity filter.

## Field-map consequence

This is an `early_signal` in **Verification & Recovery**, also touching Data Discovery & Grounding. Systems should report answer correctness, entity discipline, and provenance replay separately; one narrow benchmark does not change the stable map.
