# Chinese-First Bilingual Publication Contract

Data Agent Radar is bilingual, with Simplified Chinese as the default reader experience.

- `README.md` is Simplified Chinese default.
- `README.en.md` is the complete English counterpart.
- Reader-facing Research Library pages, high-value paper notes, and future public compactions should have both languages.
- Canonical data and maintenance contracts remain single-source. Public accepted projections are the paired reading surfaces and any due closed-period digest; the atomic Git commit is their maintenance provenance.

Candidate inventories, source-lane failures, retries, blockers, validation output, and all other operational traces are not public publication artifacts. Keep them only in ignored `.radar-private/` or ephemeral Agent memory.

Chinese and English derive from one semantic research judgment: paper identity, importance, research delta, mechanism, closest comparison, decisive evidence, caveat, field-map consequence, and links. Material changes update both high-visibility variants in the same transaction.

Keep paper/dataset/model/metric/SQL/tool/protocol names in canonical English when that improves precision and literature search. Chinese should use natural technical Chinese rather than translated English syntax; English should preserve the same depth rather than becoming a shortened translation.
