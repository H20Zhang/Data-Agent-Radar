Provenance becomes an executable same-model answer-reproduction witness rather than a relevance hint, but reproducing a wrong answer is still not correctness or business truth.
- **`new_signal` · evidence-gated-catalog-enrichment** — Support: [TRACE](../../papers/2608.20844.en.md); confidence: **medium**. - **`new_signal` · ontology-traceable-enterprise-evidence** — Support: [KDAF](../../papers/2608.20661.en.md); confidence: **medium**. Immutable execution bundles still require statistical, measurement, and claim-scope review; package-level controls and an observed reviewer miss bound causal and reliability claims about automated governance.

### Execution / Grounding interfaces

- **`new_signal` · auditable-computational-campaign** — Support: [MAESTRO](../../papers/2608.20434.en.md); confidence: **medium**. Typed state connects large-scale scientific-data curation and multi-fidelity deterministic tools into a replayable campaign; the independent gain of multi-agent/reflection and full compute cost remain unisolated.
- **`new_signal` · dataset-snapshot-skill-interface** — Support: [Scientific Data Skills](../../papers/2608.19625.en.md); confidence: **low**. ## Cross-direction synthesis (not a trend claim)

The most important shared question this week is not one agent architecture but **where the correctness contract belongs**. BLIP, KDAF, TRACE, Bolo, and Brain Researcher respectively test answer reproduction, evidence attribution, production write authorization, runnable/dataflow admission, and scientific claim admissibility. Those measurement objects are not interchangeable. Collapsing them into one “grounded / verified” score hides both false success and over-rejection.

The planning side also raises a stricter baseline requirement. ReAct-SQL shows that `adaptive probing + typed deterministic substrate` can explain substantial gains by itself; the VAT sweep provides no multiplicity-corrected evidence that more workers are better; and Artic packages explicit artifacts, routing, gates, and retry together. The next orchestration or multi-agent claim should therefore hold the model, interface, budget, and verification surface fixed while changing one control boundary.

SciDSK and MAESTRO further show why cost accounting cannot stop at online tokens: manual semantic curation, snapshot maintenance, deterministic scientific compute, trace storage, and downstream verification all belong to the full data-work loop.
