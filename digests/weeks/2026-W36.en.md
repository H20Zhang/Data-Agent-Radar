## Cross-direction synthesis (not a trend claim)

W36 has only one native acceptance, so the useful conclusion is not that structured reuse is now a trend. The sharper question is the **break-even contract for reuse**. Agentic Data Cracking establishes a narrower point: when different queries repeatedly reach the same long documents, an already-paid document prefill can be converted into persistent partial structure, trading bounded extra decode now for fewer raw-document reads later. The same experiment also reports a **1.24× baseline** paired-cost ratio at the 90th percentile, exposing the negative boundary when reuse does not materialize.

For Data Agents, this connects Learning & Reuse to Data Discovery & Grounding as one lifecycle decision: **whether state should persist depends on future hit probability, reacquisition cost, invalidation risk, and maintenance cost—not merely on whether the system can extract it.** Current evidence covers only part of that ledger under a static corpus and deliberately constructed locality. Enterprise use still needs staleness detection, version/permission changes, reverification, and store growth priced explicitly.
